# Synthetic Centre Policies

These policies are synthetic demo materials for EduFlow AU. They are not real centre policies and must not be treated as legal, regulatory, medical, or operational advice.

## Outdoor Play and Nature Learning Policy

Version: 2026.07

Outdoor play experiences should be planned from children's interests,
developmental needs, weather conditions, and available educator supervision.
Educators should check the play space before the activity, consider allergies
and environmental risks, and prepare inclusive alternatives for children who
need additional support.

Activity plans should identify the learning intention, materials, supervision
considerations, opportunities for child agency, and observation prompts.
Educators should keep the experience play-based and flexible rather than
requiring every child to complete the same product.

## Documentation and Learning Records Policy

Version: 2026.07

Learning records should use de-identified or synthetic information in demo
systems. Draft observations should focus on what the child did, said, explored,
or attempted, and should avoid diagnostic claims.

Educators should connect observations to learning outcomes only when there is
clear evidence in the record. Reflections should describe possible next steps
and remain open to educator review.

## Family Communication Draft Policy

Version: 2026.07

Family communication generated by EduFlow AU must remain a draft until reviewed
and approved by a teacher. Draft messages should be respectful, concise, and
free from sensitive personal information.

The system must not send messages directly to families. Any export, save, or
real-world communication action is treated as a controlled write that requires
teacher approval.
